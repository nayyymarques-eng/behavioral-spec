# Roles

Who uses the product, and what each role may see and do. In the synthetic user kit, each role has a persona sheet from interviews; here, only what the product lets each role do.

| ID | Role | Sees | Does |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Privacy lead | Every request, its due date and its history | Marks a request as answered, extends the due date, asks for proof of identity |
| R-02 | Support agent | The requests they sent, and who has them | Sends a request to the privacy lead. Nothing on the request after that |
