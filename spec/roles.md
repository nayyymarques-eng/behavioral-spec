# Roles

Who uses the product, and what each role may see and do. Each role has a persona sheet in `personas/`, built from interviews: what they need, what they fear and how they judge a screen. This file says only what the product lets each role do.

| ID | Role | Sees | Does | Persona sheet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Privacy lead | Every request, its due date and its history | Marks a request as answered, extends the due date, asks for proof of identity | `personas/privacy-lead.md` |
| R-02 | Support agent | The requests they sent, and who has them | Sends a request to the privacy lead. Nothing on the request after that | `personas/support-agent.md` |
